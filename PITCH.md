## 🚀 What is Forge OS?

Forge OS is a **Rugged Intelligent Workspace**. It combines dealer-level automotive diagnostics, multimodal AI assistance, and professional project management into a single, high-contrast, performance-oriented interface. Whether you are tracing a CAN-bus fault, managing a fleet of hardware projects, or coding custom firmware, Forge OS is your digital co-pilot.

---

## 💎 Core Features

### 1. 🧠 Multimodal AI Hub (Neural_Sync)
Forge OS isn't just "chatting" with an AI. It features a context-aware assistant powered by **Google Gemini** that understands your project, your inventory, and your vehicle's health.
*   **Specialist Modes**: Switch between modes like *Diagnostics Lead*, *Performance Tuner*, *Electrical Engineer*, and *Forge Coder*.
*   **Visual Inspection**: Use the AR scanner to identify parts, detect wear, or analyze complex wiring diagrams using AI Vision.

### 2. 🔌 Pro-Grade OBD-II Integration
Native, low-latency communication with ELM327-compatible hardware.
*   **Multiple Protocols**: Support for Web Bluetooth (BLE) and Web Serial (USB) adapters.
*   **Live Telemetry**: Real-time graphing of RPM, Boost, Fuel Trims, and more.
*   **Deep Module Scanning**: Go beyond generic engine codes. Scan ABS, Airbags, and Chassis modules.

### 3. 🏗️ Workshop Management
A zero-latency synchronization system powered by **Firebase** ensures your data is everywhere you are.
*   **Project-Specific Context**: Keep your tasks, diagnostic logs, and chats isolated by project (e.g., "78 Bronco Restoration" vs. "Custom Drone Build").
*   **Smart Inventory**: Track your gear and parts. Forge OS knows if you have the right multimeter or socket set for the job.

### 4. 🛡️ The Forge Guardian
The world's first **Auto-Healing codebase**. Forge OS includes an autonomous Python-based guardian that uses AI to identify and fix its own bugs, deprecations, and environment issues.
